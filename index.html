Party Bread: prepare, shape and arrange dough in glassware
Double Zojirushi Party Bread (vegan)
  • 320 g cashew milk
  • 520 g bread flour
  • 36 g sugar
  • 10 g salt
  • 28 g vegan butter
  • 6 g rapid-rise yeast
  • extra melted vegan butter for brushing

If the machine already contains a double batch, finish its 20-minute knead. If it contains a single batch, make a second identical single batch separately rather than adding ingredients late. Rest dough ball 10 minutes, divide into 20 portions total (~46 g each), shape and rest 10 minutes, finish shaping and brush lightly with melted butter. Arrange in greased pan(s), cover and refrigerate immediately. Skip the machine rise and bake cycles. Tomorrow remove at 11 AM, proof until puffy (allow 1½–2 hours or longer), bake at 350°F for 20–25 minutes or until golden and about 190°F inside. Check pan capacity; two pans may require separate baking or another oven. This is an adaptation of the manual.
